My work rarely starts with the question whether a document is present. It starts with what that document is supposed to prove.

## EUDR and EUTR

From legal obligation to a workable due-diligence process: collecting information, assessing risk, mitigating risk and being able to substantiate why a conclusion is justified.

## Timber legality and traceability

Where did the product actually come from? I assess supplier information, documents and transaction flows, with particular attention to whether the physical product and the administrative evidence demonstrably belong together.

## FSC{{< trademark >}} and PEFC

Chain of Custody, multisite, project certification and Controlled Wood, but also the boundary between what certification can substantiate and what legal due diligence still requires beyond it.

## Audits and systems

A system only works if it can also be tested. I design, implement and review due-diligence systems, procedures, control questions and evidence structures, with ISO 19011 as an important audit framework.
