My work focuses on the practical application of rules and controls in international timber supply chains.

## EUDR and EUTR

Analysis of obligations, due-diligence processes, information requirements, risk assessment, risk mitigation and auditable documentation.

## Timber legality and traceability

Assessment of origin, supplier information, documents, transaction flows and the link between the physical product and administrative evidence.

## FSC® and PEFC

Support on Chain of Custody, multisite and project questions, Controlled Wood and the relationship between certification and legal due diligence.

## Audits and systems

Design, implementation and review of due-diligence systems, procedures, control questions and evidence structures. ISO 19011 provides an important audit framework.

The detailed pages will progressively connect this guidance with legislation, standards, countries and source material in the knowledge graph.
