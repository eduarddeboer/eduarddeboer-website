This section brings together articles and technical analysis on subjects that recur in my work.

The focus is the practical relationship between legislation, certification, supply-chain information and evidence. Recent themes include the EUDR, EMPco, the PPWR, digital product information and situations where multiple European regulatory frameworks apply to the same product or supply chain.

Where useful, publications will link to the underlying legislation, organisations, countries and concepts in the knowledge graph.
